TBGP 2030 Vision

The Vision Goal

By 2030, TBGP aims to be a premier marketing and partnership powerhouse in the construction industry — driven by 20,000 to 30,000 Member Project Facilitators distributing opportunities to facilitators, 3,000 member contractors, service providers, suppliers, and poor Filipino families, backed by at least 10 local and foreign capital partners.
