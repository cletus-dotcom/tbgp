ISA Nalang Ang Kausap

Isa Nalang Ang Kausap

All Your Needs — Under One Roof, One Platform, One Point of Coordination

At the core of this concept is a simple idea: when you build or expand, you should not chase a dozen contacts for materials, manpower, logistics, and coordination. With TBGP, you have one trusted partner who can source, match, and manage the moving parts of your construction and supply needs.

The TBGP program is already supported by a growing nationwide and international network — more than 1,000 Project Facilitators, 400+ member contractors, and 100+ service providers and suppliers working together under one platform.

By December 2026, the network is projected to reach:

  • 2,000 Project Facilitators
  • 800–1,000 Member Contractors
  • 200+ Service Providers and Partner Manufacturer Suppliers

Membership orientations and collaboration meetings continue across the country as the TBGP network expands — so clients, facilitators, and partners always have a clear path to coordinated support.

Behind the platform is a network of thousands of Project Facilitators, hundreds of contractors, service providers, and manufacturer suppliers across the Philippines — organized so ordinary Filipinos and project owners can work with confidence.

A distinctive profit-sharing scheme directs 35% of net profit from construction projects and 45% from construction-related product sales toward benefiting ordinary Filipinos and project facilitators in the network.

Beyond sharing, TBGP invests in skills development, business training, and financial literacy — so partners grow with the platform, not only through a single transaction.

Isa Na Lang Ang Kausap means a simpler, more convenient life for clients: one point of contact, clear accountability, and business integrity from inquiry to delivery.
