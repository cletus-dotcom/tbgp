Request a project

Foundation, slope protection, site development, retrofitting, and design-and-build works delivered through accredited TBGP contractors. Tell us about your project and a project coordinator will follow up.

  1. Inquiry You send the scope, location, and target schedule. You get a PRJ reference number.
  2. Assessment A coordinator calls you, reviews plans, and schedules a site ocular when needed.
  3. Costing & quotation The technical team prepares the layout / BOQ and submits a quotation.
  4. Contractor assignment An accredited contractor is endorsed and assigned to deliver the works.
  5. Implementation The coordinator monitors the project until completion.
