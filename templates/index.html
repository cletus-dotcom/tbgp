TBGP

Mission Statement

To get the attention of every stakeholder in the Philippine Construction Industry—bringing together Project Owners, Developers, Contractors, Service Providers, Suppliers, Investors, Project Funders, and Ordinary Filipinos nationwide to work together and call for UNITY through INTEGRITY, COOPERATION, and BUSINESS COLLABORATION.

To make every Client’s life and project easier through “ISA NA LANG ANG KAUSAP”—providing one trusted point of coordination while TBGP manages the necessary coordination with Contractors, Suppliers, Service Providers, and other project partners, giving Clients a simpler, more convenient, and more comfortable project experience.

To help STOP and prevent the perennial problem faced by Clients including the REAL ESTATE DEVELOPERS’ dealing with erring Contractors who fail to complete their works, abandon their obligations, or leave projects unfinished after receiving payments, while promoting the TBGP COMMITMENT to building a HIGH-INTEGRITY CONSTRUCTION PROGRAM where every project is managed with TRANSPARENCY, ACCOUNTABILITY, and RESPONSIBILITY—protecting the TRUST, INVESTMENT, and INTERESTS of every Client through the newly formulated and first-ever TBGP SECURED PROJECT IMPLEMENTATION SYSTEM, specifically designed to protect the project, the Client, and the successful completion of every undertaking.

At the same time, to promote at the highest level of awareness to all construction industry stakeholders the BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES that the TBGP PROGRAM has established for every FILIPINO, and to SHARE the truth and reality behind TBGP’s National Campaign:

“SAMA-SAMA TAYO UPANG IANGAT ANG BUHAY NG BAWAT ISANG NAGHIHIRAP NA PILIPINO.”

Our Mission

TBGP

Vision Statement

Imagine a Philippines where the entire Construction Industry, Government, Business Sector, and Ordinary Filipinos work together through the TBGP Program—united by Integrity, Transparency, Accountability, Cooperation, and Responsibility.

Imagine when Municipal, Provincial, and National Leaders, together with the Private and Government Sectors, support this concept, and its principles become part of the laws and policies of the land.

This is the Philippines TBGP envisions—a more beautiful, more balanced, and more prosperous nation, where projects are properly implemented, opportunities are shared, communities are uplifted, and the benefits of development reach Ordinary Filipinos and the Poorest of the Poor.

Making this vision possible is TBGP’s existing Business Profit-Sharing Scheme, where 35% to 45% of the profit from TBGP projects is dedicated to benefiting Ordinary Filipinos and the Poorest of the Poor.

Turning business success into shared opportunity, community development, and a better future for the Philippines.

Values Statement

TBGP believes that HOW WE DO BUSINESS IS AS IMPORTANT AS THE BUSINESS ITSELF.

We are committed to INTEGRITY, HONESTY, FAIRNESS, RESPECT, HUMILITY, GOOD ATTITUDE, UNITY, and ACCOUNTABILITY in every relationship and transaction.

We are NON-DISCRIMINATORY, NON-POLITICAL, and NON-RELIGIOUS, respecting every person regardless of status, background, or beliefs. NO LYING. NO ARROGANCE.

NO DISCRIMINATION. We believe in treating everyone fairly, working together, and doing what is right.

Integrity is our standard. Unity is our strength. Respect is for everyone.
